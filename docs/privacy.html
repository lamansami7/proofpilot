ProofPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

ProofPilot ("ProofPilot", "we", "us", or "our") is a purchase-protection application designed to help users organize purchase records, receipts, warranties, return deadlines, documents, and claims.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ProofPilot, the application may process information that you provide, including purchase information, receipts, documents, warranty information, return deadlines, and claim-related information.

Account information may include information necessary to authenticate your account, such as your email address.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and maintain ProofPilot, synchronize your information when synchronization is enabled, help you organize purchases and documents, provide requested features, protect the security of the service, and respond to support requests.

Documents and Receipts

ProofPilot may allow you to store receipts and other documents. You should only upload information that you are comfortable storing through the service.

AI Features

Some ProofPilot features may use artificial intelligence to process information you submit in order to provide requested functionality. Information sent to an AI provider depends on the feature you use and the configuration of the service.

Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed by ProofPilot. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Deletion

ProofPilot provides account and data deletion functionality where supported by the service. Users may also contact us using the support address below regarding privacy-related requests.

Third-Party Services

ProofPilot may use third-party infrastructure and service providers to provide authentication, database, storage, AI, hosting, analytics, or other application functionality.

Children's Privacy

ProofPilot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our service, practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
